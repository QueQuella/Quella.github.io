Interactive art · Deep learning · In Development

Programming Dream

A compact interactive experiment set around a floating computer on a dreamlike sea. The participant draws a pattern that a planned CNN model interprets as one of six creature labels, linking human imagination with machine classification. PyTorch learning and Unreal Engine integration are being developed alongside the scene and interface.

Images

Visual studies in environment design, illustration and narrative planning.

Scenery / Environment Studies

Using Blender, I study how modelling, materials and lighting create atmosphere in cave, historical-inspired and snowy environments. These exercises develop my understanding of composition, spatial depth and the relationship between colour and mood.

Illustration & Character Studies

Digital illustration and character design use Photoshop and Clip Studio Paint to explore silhouettes, colour and visual identity. Scene illustrations, cover design and character studies help me translate narrative ideas into readable images.

Scripts & Storyboards

Script presentations, storyboard panels, a music-video storyline and an adaptation treatment record how I plan visual narratives. They develop my ability to organise shots, pacing and transitions before producing a moving-image work.

Videos

Animation exercises, responsive imagery and moving-image experiments.

Animation Studies

2D animation fundamentals, walk and run cycles, blendshape studies and The Rift Park develop skills in timing, spacing, weight and expressive motion. Working across 2D practice, Maya and 3D animation also introduces simulation and character movement as ways to tell visual stories.

Interactive & Real-time Studies

Portrait Interaction and Music Interaction use Processing to explore responses between image, input and sound. The UE5 HLSL experiment studies real-time visual effects and connects technical experiments with visual feedback. These projects connect visual outcomes with the systems that produce them.

Video Experiments

Children of the Machine explores AI-assisted moving images in a music-video format. Video editing practice develops sequencing, rhythm and the relationship between sound and image, while encouraging reflection on how generated material is shaped into a visual narrative.

Media & Communication Theory

Course writing and reading responses examine communication, media and visual culture. Reading: Internet Art connects this theoretical background with digital creative practice and the ways artworks operate through networked media.

Game Studies

Reading responses and project research consider how interactive works organise participation, identity and choice. Unstable Aesthetics: Game sits alongside the developing narrative questions of Lakespeaker: Soliloquy, linking critical reading with design practice.

AI & Digital Media

AI Art — Reading Response records study of the first two chapters of AI Art. This category considers machine creativity and computational interpretation, alongside questions raised by the relationship between human expression and emerging media.

Experiments

Creative coding and real-time studies use Processing, TouchDesigner and Unreal Engine to investigate responsive images and interactive environments. Programming Dream extends this learning into dataset design, CNN classification and data augmentation with PyTorch; model evaluation and integration remain in development.
