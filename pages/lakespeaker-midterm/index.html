GAME CONCEPT

Why Create This Game?

Begin with my personal experiences, as a game creator, I find myself less interested in completing large-scale games. And I prefer games that cover exploration, emotional experiences, and the mysteries that make players think. During my university years, I often question my own identity and circumstances. In a summer holiday, after rewatching the TV adaptations of The Lost Tomb (盗墓笔记), I'm fascinated by the atmosphere of entering unknown spaces and the mystery of the Bronze Gate, where fate and destiny seem deeply connected. These experiences inspire me to begin creating this game.

How to Play?

The player takes the role of Lin Shen (林深), a 28-year-old postgraduate student in folklore studies. As a child, he was told by an old fortune-teller that he was the "Heir of the Lake". He has always been uncertain about this prophecy, until he accidentally drowns during a field investigation by the lake. And he is trapped in his own dream.

To find the exits, players go through three levels: finding memories, exploring beliefs, and making choices. The ultimate goal is to escape the dream, build own narrative and choose the self.

What Will Players Experience?

This game takes players through a journey of uncertainty, fate, and personal choice. Ultimately, I want players to experience a rich narrative journey that leaves them with personal emotions, memories, and questions to reflect on, even after the game ends.

GAME MECHANICS

Player Control

From character model to animation blueprint, I use the rigged girl model that was completed in 3D animation course. Players can press Q to shift perspectives, press shift to switch state between walking and running, press 1 to turn on the flashlight.

Problem: When the players rotate the viewpoint, they can see inside the model and it looks scary and blocks the sight.

Solution: Modify the character's material that can become transparent within certain distance.

Interaction Systems

Computer System

Location: Level 1, in the secret room

This computer works for the web searching and email checking. Players should find fragmented memories and get a message: the owner of this computer goes to the same place every year. And, players can guess and find hints to search for the key clues, like video, journal or diary, etc. This is where players can get more information and background stories.

Door Interaction

Location: Bedroom and secret room

The door with lock uses toon shader and animation transition to automatically open. And the lock's password answer "GAZE" would be related to the hidden gaze system. (Players can accidentally gaze at mirrors for more than 3s to trigger the dialogue system, and make choices to influence the ending outcome.)
