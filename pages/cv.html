01 / Education

I study Animation and Interactive Design in the Department of Communication and Film at BNBU (2023–2027). I see myself as a multimedia creator, working across animation, games, moving images and interactive design. My interests in psychology and philosophy shape the questions I explore through my work: how we perceive the world, construct memories and understand ourselves. I bring these interests into visual storytelling, imagined environments and participatory experiences, combining creative practice with research and experimentation.
