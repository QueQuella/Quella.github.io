Final Year Project / In Development

Lakespeaker: Soliloquy

An interactive narrative exploring identity, memory and choice in a dreamlike landscape.

01 / Concept

Seeking a self

The player follows traces of memory through an atmospheric world. The central question is how a person understands and shapes their identity while meeting stories told by other people and by the environment.

02 / Narrative framework

Internal & external narratives

Internal narrative is the character's subjective memory and interpretation. External narrative arrives through people, social accounts and environmental clues. The player's response to both becomes part of the character's developing sense of self.

03 / Player journey

Explore · Interpret · Choose

The current framework moves from seeking the self to reconstructing it, then choosing how to respond to conflicting accounts. Formative choices and a potential critical choice are in narrative development; the final structure and endings have not been fixed.

04 / World

Environment & character

The project uses a dreamlike journey and lakeside setting to place narrative clues in spaces the player explores. Character prototypes, environmental studies and level design will be documented here as the project develops.

05 / Systems

Development notes

An identity state system and AI-mediated dialogue are planned areas of investigation. The dialogue system is a proposal for presenting external narratives, not a completed feature. Further screenshots, playable evidence and design notes will be added as they become available.
