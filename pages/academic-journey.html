2025–2026 / Year 3 · Semester 2

Y3S2

Animation Sound Design and Mixing

This course developed my understanding of sound design for animation, including field recording, Foley, ambience, atmosphere, and spatial audio. I explored microphone polar patterns, mono and stereo recording techniques (AB, XY, ORTF, and Mid-Side), and professional audio editing and mixing workflows using Pro Tools. Through these studies, I learned how to construct immersive soundscapes, balance dialogue and sound effects, and use sound as a creative tool for visual storytelling.

Communication Research Methods

This course strengthened my understanding of systematic research in media and communication, covering research design, sampling, measurement, and research ethics. I explored both quantitative and qualitative methods, including surveys, experiments, content analysis, in-depth interviews, focus groups, and ethnography, alongside basic statistical analysis. Through these studies, I developed a more critical and evidence-based approach to investigating media audiences, communication behaviors, and the relationship between media and society.

Multimedia Storytelling

This course compares creative media and narrative patterns across music videos, animation, experimental video art, video games and commercials.

01 / Adaptation to a music video

I created a treatment for a music video promoting Children of the Machine. I used AI tools to visualise my ideas.

Watch the MV →

02 / Book adaptation

I adapted Borges’s The Circular Ruins from a short story into visual media. I explored challenges such as dealing with nonlinear time, building a 3D fantasy world and combining 3D with 2D drawing.

Watch the adaptation demo →

03 / Adaptation for a game

I developed a spin-off of the video game Outer Wilds titled Weaver of Trinity, a first-person 3D RPG that tests my ability to research, analyse and create a narrative within the video game market.

04 / Commercial advertisement

For this group project, I created a stop-motion public service announcement titled Protect Birds. We used paper cuttings, cardboard and multiple layers of boards.
