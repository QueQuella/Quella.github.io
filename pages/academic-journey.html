Media History

Through this assignment, I learned to build a focused critical argument about an anime, game or film, using close reading for a single work or comparison across several works. Reading a wide range of articles on film, anime, games and media gave me a theoretical foundation for exploring questions of identity, spectatorship, narrative and play. This course helped me connect creative interests with historical contexts and develop a more rigorous approach to media analysis.

Advanced Interactive Media Workshop / UE5.6

This course focused on using Unreal Engine’s Material Editor to create procedural and animated shaders through both node-based workflows and custom HLSL code. I learned core shader concepts, including UV manipulation, mathematical operations for shape generation, and the use of loops and time-based functions to produce dynamic visual effects.

Final project

The Last Gate

We recreated the battlefield after a siege of a medieval castle, with cannons destroying the city wall. The scene used Niagara particles and destruction systems to convey the brutality of war and the desolation of the battlefield.

The sequence begins with a wide view of the war, moves into details of the city gate and artillery attack, and concludes with cannon fire destroying the wall.

Animation Sound Design and Mixing

This course developed my understanding of sound design for animation, including field recording, Foley, ambience, atmosphere, and spatial audio. I explored microphone polar patterns, mono and stereo recording techniques (AB, XY, ORTF, and Mid-Side), and professional audio editing and mixing workflows using Pro Tools. Through these studies, I learned how to construct immersive soundscapes, balance dialogue and sound effects, and use sound as a creative tool for visual storytelling.

2024 Fall–2025 Spring / Year 2

Y2

History of Animation

Through this course, I developed an understanding of animation’s historical evolution, from early optical devices and motion experiments to the emergence of major animation studios. I explored techniques such as rotoscoping, stop-motion, multi-plane cameras and character animation, alongside the artistic, technological and industrial developments that shaped the medium.

Introduction to Communication Theory

Through this course, I learned the foundational concepts of communication as a relational process of creating, interpreting and responding to messages. I learned to apply major communication theories, such as Social Penetration Theory, the Elaboration Likelihood Model, Cultivation Theory and the Technology Acceptance Model, to various media contexts including anime, video games and VR/AR. Overall, I developed a keen sense for media and the ability to analyse its essence.

2D Animation

Through this course, I developed a foundation in traditional 2D animation, focusing on timing, spacing, keyframes, in-betweens and the principles of motion. I explored techniques such as squash and stretch, anticipation, follow-through, overlapping action and exaggeration to create expressive and believable character movement. I also learned the animation production pipeline, from storyboarding and timing sheets to frame-by-frame animation, colouring and final compositing, strengthening my understanding of visual storytelling, movement and character performance.

Interactive Graphics

Through this Processing course, I developed a practical foundation in creative coding by learning how to use loops, arrays, functions, classes and noise to build interactive graphics and generative visual systems. I applied these technical skills in projects such as music visualization, where I explored how sound can be translated into dynamic motion, rhythm and abstract visual forms. I also strengthened my understanding of coding structure, debugging and visual experimentation, while gaining a more artistic sense of how programming can be used as a medium for expressive and interactive design.

01 / Processing study

Self-Portrait

I first transformed the picture into a mosaic, using a technique introduced in class. I then adjusted the background’s grayscale and added a randomly moving crosshair to create a mysterious, detective-like atmosphere. To introduce interaction, I used a circular mask controlled by the mouse. Randomly distributed iridescent pink accents added colour variation and spatial definition, while typographic elements balanced the composition.

02 / Processing study

Music Visualization

I began by setting up the audio and particle systems, then analysed the audio spectrum and waveform continuously in real time. The data generated a rotating, ring-shaped waveform visualization. Audio parameters also controlled the 3D rotation angles and particle movement, including speed and direction. Finally, I rendered the 3D particle system and repeated these steps throughout playback.

3D Modelling

Through this course, I developed a solid foundation in Autodesk Maya, exploring polygonal and NURBS modelling, digital sculpting, Boolean operations, deformers and UV unwrapping. I learned to create realistic materials through texture mapping, normal and bump maps, transparency and shading, alongside lighting, camera composition and rendering using Arnold. Through object modelling and environment design projects, I gained experience in the complete 3D modelling pipeline, from concept sketches and reference studies to modelling, texturing, lighting and final rendering.

2023 Fall–2024 Spring / Year 1

Y1

Media Aesthetics

Through this course, I developed a deeper understanding of film aesthetics and cinematic form, exploring how narrative, cinematography, composition, lighting, color, sound, and editing shape visual meaning and audience perception. I engaged with film theories from André Bazin, Rudolf Arnheim, and Sergei Eisenstein, examining different perspectives on realism, artistic expression, and montage. The course strengthened my ability to critically analyze films beyond their narratives.

I also practiced illustration and visual expression, strengthening my ability to translate observations and creative ideas into drawings.
