Core Concept

This game combines psychological elements with a magical realist art style to create an immersive narrative puzzle experience. Players reveal characters’ inner worlds and growth stories through puzzle-solving, interaction, and exploration within detailed and exquisite 3D box garden environments.

My Contribution

I planned the main maze level and character design, implemented text dialogues and scene transitions, and designed the promotional poster.
