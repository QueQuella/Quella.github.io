OneTwoThe

An Interactive Visual Novel

I wrote the first playable version in Twine using SugarCube to track what the player had discovered behind each door. Those choices shaped the script for the visual novel in Unreal Engine 5. I used Unreal Motion Graphics (UMG) widgets to present dialogue and choices over illustrated scenes, turning a passage link in the prototype into an on-screen action, such as reaching for a door handle.

Genre:
First-person psychological mystery
Format:
Branching visual novel
Prototype:
Twine / SugarCube
Engine:
Unreal Engine 5 / UMG

Play the Twine prototype

You wake in a medical room with no memory of the woman beside you. One door leads into a forest. The other opens onto a dark space filled with screens. You can follow both paths, but you can also decide you have seen enough. Across four endings, what counts as a happy ending is yours to judge.
