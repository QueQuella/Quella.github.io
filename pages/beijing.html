A digital heritage travelogue from a six-day field trip to Beijing. A film introduces the city, a book gathers the places we visited, and a chapter on Zhoukoudian lets visitors move between the scale of an excavation and the detail of a single skull.

From field trip to field notes

What stays with you after visiting a heritage site? I wanted the small observations from our trip to sit alongside the archaeological material. Moving images recall the journey, while the slower pace of turning a page gives each place room for a closer look.

Inside the heritage book

Turn through four chapters or use the index to jump to a spread. Zhoukoudian is the developed study. Open it to look around the excavation model, inspect the skull, and read the accompanying historical notes. The other spreads sketch out how the collection can grow.

Working with digital scans

I used Three.js to bring the book and GLB models into the browser. The point-cloud view reveals the shape of a scan through individual samples. As a surface forms, details become easier to read. Rotating the model lets the visitor choose which details to examine.
